The three layers

  • 1 · Scan scan.py: sorts every branch into scalar, vec1, jagged, jagged-deep, empty or unreadable, and writes a manifest to audit.
  • 2 · Convert convert.py: flattens to CSV and parquet, in one quick pass or following the manifest's policy for jagged branches.
  • 3 · Filter (optional) dropfilter.py: drops the branches in drop_list.txt from the parquet without reading the ROOT file again.

Quick start: python3 convert.py /path/to/file.root. Nothing on this sheet runs the tool; the dashboard reads its CSV output through the locations file.
