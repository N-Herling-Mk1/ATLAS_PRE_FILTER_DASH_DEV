What the numbers under each plot are
mean, var, std
Per class, on valid values only: NaN, inf and sentinels (−999, plus any ruled on the Verdict board) are removed and counted as invalid. Unweighted; signal is the four mass points pooled.
MaxEnt case
What the column is, and so which least-committed models are allowed: binary (Bernoulli), an integer lattice (PMFs only), or a density on R, R+ or [0, 1] — with a hurdle when one value holds 5% or more of the column, since no smooth density can put weight on a single value.
ladder, best
Each case has a ladder of constraint sets (Gaussian, gamma, lognormal, quartic, COM-Poisson, …). Every rung is fitted to both classes on the same rows; the best is the lowest BIC. Rungs within ΔBIC 2 of the best are indistinguishable — the column is then not identified at the family level.
left out
Nats of information the best fit does not carry: the data's cross-entropy under the fit minus its own entropy. On the Gaussian rung this is the negentropy J.
captures
Bhattacharyya distance between the S and B best fits, as a share of the histogram distance. Next to it, the same share for the Gaussian pair: when MaxEnt captures much more than the Gaussian, the separation lives in shape.
Gaussian ref
J = HG − H per class: how far each class is from the Gaussian at its own variance.
DB hist / null
Bhattacharyya distance between the two histograms, tails included, and its mean over 20 label permutations — the level noise alone reaches. Compare against the null, not zero. Disjoint supports are flagged as a leakage signature.
discrete
Counts and flags get lattice fits only: a Gaussian is a density and these are PMFs.
